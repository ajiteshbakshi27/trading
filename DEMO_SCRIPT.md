# Parakh — hackathon demo script (3 minutes)

Read this out loud twice before the event. Everything below works without
logging in, so nothing can fail on a password.

## Before you go on stage
- Open the site 5 minutes early (the free server needs a minute to wake).
- Open three browser tabs, in this order: `/xray`, `/try`, `/sample-score`.
- In the `/xray` tab, click **Use a sample tradebook** once to warm it up, then reload.
- Laptop on charger, notifications off, browser zoom at 125%.
- Have the backup video ready (see the end of this file).

## The script

**0:00 — The problem (20 seconds, no screen needed)**
> "SEBI's own study found that about 9 out of 10 individual traders in
> futures and options lose money. They do not need more tips. Tips are the
> problem. What nobody gives them is honest feedback on their own trading.
> That is Parakh. Parakh means 'to test'."

**0:20 — Tradebook X-ray (60 seconds) → tab 1**
Click **Use a sample tradebook**.
> "A trader uploads the trade file their broker already gives them. This is
> a sample file with invented trades, and the page says so."

Point at the big headline.
> "In two seconds: this trader lost about 14,000 rupees overall, and almost
> all of it, 13,979 rupees, came from one habit. Trades taken within 30
> minutes of a loss. Revenge trading. Their other trades roughly broke even."

Point at "Not a problem" card.
> "It also says where there is no problem. Trading in the opening rush is
> fine for this trader. And it never says 'you would have saved this money'.
> It only shows where the losses sit. We do not claim what we cannot prove."

**1:20 — Check a trade idea (50 seconds) → tab 2**
Click the first example, then **Check it**.
> "Before the next trade, the trader types the idea in plain language.
> Parakh reads it with AI, pulls live NSE prices, and tests it."

Point at the tag "Live data", then the checklist, then the odds bar.
> "A checklist with red flags. The trader's own confidence next to what
> actually happened in similar past setups. And 'what would prove you
> wrong', written before the money goes in. No buy or sell call anywhere.
> We test the trader's idea. We do not give ours."

**2:10 — The score (30 seconds) → tab 3**
> "Every saved idea is tracked against real prices automatically. Over time
> the trader gets this. When this sample trader says 90% sure, they are
> right half the time. This is the number a trader never sees anywhere else."

**2:40 — Close (20 seconds)**
> "Parakh is not an adviser and gives no tips, which also keeps it clear of
> SEBI's adviser rules. It is a mirror. Free to try without signing up.
> The paid plan will add broker sync and unlimited checks. Thank you."

## Likely judge questions

**Is this investment advice? Do you need a SEBI licence?**
No buy, sell or hold calls and no price targets of our own. The user brings
the idea; we test it and keep their journal. We will take a lawyer's opinion
before a public launch.

**Where does the data come from?**
Live NSE prices through Yahoo Finance for now, labelled "Live data" on screen.
For a commercial launch we will buy licensed exchange data.

**What does the AI do?**
Two things only: it turns the trader's sentence into a structured idea, and it
answers questions about the trader's own records. The numbers (odds, checks,
X-ray) are plain maths, so the AI cannot make them up.

**How is this different from a trading journal app?**
Journals show what happened. Parakh tests the idea before the trade, shows
habits in rupees, and scores whether confidence matches results.

**How do you make money?**
Free: X-ray and a few checks. Pro at about ₹499 a month: broker sync,
unlimited checks, the copilot, alerts. Later: a dashboard for trading
educators and their students.

**How many users do you have?**
Say the true number. If it is zero, say: "None yet. This is what we built
first; next is 10 real traders."

**Is the X-ray proof that the habit caused the loss?**
No, and the screen says so. It shows where losses cluster. Each habit needs
at least 5 trades, and the whole report needs at least 20 closed trades.

## Do not say
- "Parakh predicts the market" or "improves your returns". It does neither.
- Any accuracy percentage for Parakh itself. We have not measured one.
- "AI-powered trading". The trader trades. Parakh checks.
